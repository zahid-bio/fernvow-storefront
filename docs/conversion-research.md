# Fernvow conversion research

Reviewed November 19, 2026. The goal is to help shoppers who are ready to buy choose a formula, understand what they will pay, and complete their order. Fernvow is in preview, so this pass has usability checks and no live sales baseline.

## What the evidence supports

| Buyer decision | Research | Applied to Fernvow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Will this work for my routine?” | In Baymard's product-page usability testing, 56% explored images first. Jiang and Benbasat's study found that how useful a presentation felt for evaluating a product helped explain purchase intentions. The first result is observed behavior in usability sessions. The second measures intentions. [Baymard product imagery study](https://baymard.com/research-articles/product-images-descriptive-text) · [Jiang and Benbasat](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/isre.1070.0124) | The product and texture photos sit beside concise finish, skin type, size and fragrance details. Catalog cards show size and scent for comparison. The illustrated application instructions and full ingredient lists are available on the product page. |
| “What do I do when I'm ready?” | Baymard recommends a distinct **Add to Cart** action, with price and product choices close to it. Its UX audit guidance is based on observed friction in shopping tasks. [Baymard buy-section guidance](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-ux-audit) · [Baymard research methodology](https://baymard.com/research/methodology) | The lower, photo-led product section carries one prominent **Add to bag** button after the usage instructions. It displays the selected quantity's price. The quantity selector updates both purchase buttons, and the bag confirms the items added. |
| “What will it cost to get here, and what if it doesn't suit me?” | Baymard's 2026 roundup reports that 42% of surveyed US online shoppers had abandoned a cart while browsing or before they were ready to buy. Among the remaining reasons, 40% cited high extra costs and 12% cited difficulty seeing the total upfront. In a separate study of 1,032 respondents, 60% reported looking for the return policy on the product page. These are survey findings across stores. [Baymard abandonment survey](https://baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate) · [Baymard product-page return research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux) | Delivery and returns links sit beside the primary purchase controls. Preview policies are labeled as drafts. Real shipping quotes, delivery estimates and approved terms belong here once the checkout can calculate or support them. |
| “Can I finish the order easily?” | Baymard observed users overlook guest checkout when stores buried it below other options. Its abandonment survey also reports account requirements and checkout complexity as separate reasons among shoppers who were ready to buy. [Baymard guest-checkout findings](https://baymard.com/research-articles/make-guest-checkout-prominent) · [Baymard abandonment survey](https://baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate) | The bag shows quantities and subtotal, with a primary checkout action and a separate way back to browsing. When the payment gateway is connected, verify that guest buyers can complete the hosted checkout and that The Daily Duo's advertised savings match the amount charged. |
| “Can I trust what I see?” | Research on book retailers links customer reviews to relative sales, with effects specific to the setting. A separate randomized experiment with 10,000 UK participants found manipulated reviews steered some buyers toward an inferior option. [Chevalier and Mayzlin](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10148) · [Akesson and colleagues](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31836/w31836.pdf) | Use the existing ingredient lists, product photos, sizes and catalog pricing as the initial evidence. Collect reviews from actual buyers once orders are being fulfilled. |
| “Does the page respond when I try to buy?” | Vodafone reported more sales in a landing-page A/B test that improved field LCP. The effect size belongs to that site and treatment. Google's engineering guidance recommends prioritizing the main image, reserving space for images and lowering the priority of inactive slides. [Vodafone experiment](https://web.dev/case-studies/vodafone) · [Google LCP guidance](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-lcp) · [Google CLS guidance](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-cls) | Hero and main product images load with high priority. Below-the-fold photos are lazy-loaded, and inactive carousel images use low fetch priority. Measure performance again on the hosted domain under real mobile conditions. |

## From preview to paid orders

1. **Payments.** Connect and test the gateway in the existing checkout flow. Verify a single-item order, The Daily Duo price, shipping and tax, any accepted wallets, an unsuccessful payment, the order confirmation, and a refund. Confirm the shipping and return policies reflect the real fulfillment process. See the [payment setup guide](payments-setup.md).
2. **Domain.** Run the same test order from the custom domain. Check guest checkout on mobile, accurate links to policies and customer support, and consistency between catalog, bag and payable total.
3. **Ads and measurement.** Once real orders work, record product views, add-to-bag actions, checkout starts and completed paid orders. Count a purchase using the order record and deduplicate it by order ID. Review purchase rate and revenue per visitor by landing page, device and campaign. Use add-to-bag and checkout rates to locate friction between steps.

For the first experiment, compare the lower section's benefit headline with a short description of who the formula suits. Keep the photo, price and purchase button the same. Assign visitors consistently and measure the share of eligible visitors who place at least one paid order. Track returns and revenue per visitor alongside that metric. Set the minimum change worth detecting before the test and wait for the planned sample. At a hypothetical 3% baseline, detecting a change to 3.6% with 80% power and a two-sided 5% significance level takes about 14,000 independent visitors per variant with an equal split. At early traffic levels, observe shoppers completing tasks and review funnel counts before drawing causal conclusions. [Microsoft research on controlled experiments](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/ftir-online-evaluation-final-journal.pdf)
